  votes, market and price events, MULE destruction and tile changes, personal cash events.

**Thin baseline (spec §0.1):** 4 colony-wide events, no per-plot lines · about 1 day · Heiko wants events animated on the
map, and a colony-wide event without per-plot hits and an origin gives WS6 nothing to draw
→ **PR-1 relationship:** the baseline lost for a true reason (Heiko's words, via the PM); PR-1 is the shortest slice
through the chosen approach: all 10 events and the contract, because the contract is what the screens consume.

**Constraints:** spec §0.2 (HC-1 … HC-6) travel with every task.

**Architecture:** a pure, seeded planner (`events.ts`) picks the month's event and its per-plot changes; production
records every plot's output as parts, applies the event last and returns event and lines; the month report carries
them (two named edits in WS3's files); what-if copies never roll.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, vitest, the headless simulator.
