## Goals — lifted from spec §0. Reference, do not restate.

| Goal | Who benefits | Value delivered | Proof | First delivered in |
|------|--------------|-----------------|-------|--------------------|

| MG-2 [spec §0] | the family; Heiko judging by eye | each game starts different and readable; special fields and scarcity to fight over | UBER-AC-2 | **PR-2** |

**Why no goal lands in PR-1:** PR-1 is the rules checker. It's independently useful the moment it lands: it measures any map,
including today's, for the gallery Heiko judges and for WS1. It's also what PR-2's CI test and the gallery stand on (it
unblocks two later deliverables). The first map a player receives that meets the rules comes from PR-2, and both goals land there.

**Non-goals (spec §0), lifted verbatim. A task serving one is cut:**
- Super tiles: only their rule is reserved (RULE-1 row X1). Built later, with politics and their counters.
- Unexplored map edges, roads, outposts, and the other five special fields (outpost, spaceport pad, federation beacon,
  old road, creature lair).
- The classic 9×5 map; map styles (archipelago and so on).
- Fixing colonist hunger: it does not track map ground (about 90% of games are hungry whatever the map). WS1's item.
- Changing the 10 balance checks, or tuning any non-map number.
- A river that forks or loops back (needs a renderer change in WS4's package).

**Thin baseline (spec §0.1):** patch today's nearest-seed generator · about half the code · loses because today's layout
leaves hills and mountains off 8% of four-player maps, so the Smithore floor could only be met by lifting plains or forest
past what their hints allow (breaks S9).
→ **PR-1 relationship:** the baseline lost for a true reason (re-checked at plan time: `maps-baseline.ts` still prints the
no-hills share on the current trunk, since map.ts is unchanged since e4785fc). PR-1 is the checker the chosen approach is
measured by; PR-2 is the shortest slice that delivers both goals.

**Constraints:** spec §0.2 (HC-1 … HC-7) travel with every task.

**Architecture:** `generateMap` becomes an orchestrator over pure stages in `map-layout.ts` (river, budget, regions) and
`map-ground.ts` (dealing, rings, floors, reach, seams, scarcity), all tuned from one `MAP` block in config.ts. One pure
checker in `map-rules.ts` is what the CI test, the gallery and the stage tests all call. Special fields (`specials.ts`)
come after, with their effects inside `rawOutput`/`factoryFit` so the AI values them unchanged.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, vitest, pnpm workspaces; the gallery is a Vite + React mock using `@mule/ui`'s HoloMap.
