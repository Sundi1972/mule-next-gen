### Proposed spec amendments (for WS1 to review and the PM to rule; the plan already follows them)

- **AM-1 (D-1, C1, C4, §3):** WS2's #227 on `ws2-brain` consumes
  `PlaybookSource { draft(state, seatId) → { playbook, land: LandDraft }; validate: ValidatePlan }`
  (`apps/server/src/brain/playbooks.ts`), with `playbook.phase = 'landGrant'`. PR-1 keeps the spec's seat API and adds
  `playbookSource(seats)` in `playbook/source.ts`, which returns exactly that shape. Its draft is the seat's planning
  draft with only the strategy and the landGrant part, phase `'landGrant'`. Its validate dispatches to the seat's own
  validator. `ValidatePlan` is declared in `playbook/` with the same structure as WS2's, because WS2's type exists only on
  `ws2-brain` and INV-2 forbids importing from `llm/`. `LandDraft` is declared the same way.
- **AM-2 (D-2, C9, C11, F-1, B-AC-9):** `MuleIntent` has 21 commands and `AiAction` has 14, with `walkStep` and
  `lineTrade` new since grounding. The coverage table gains a row for each: `lineTrade` maps to the AI move, and
  `walkStep` maps to the `walk` move, the tap-step form of the same walk. That mapping is proposed; WS1 rules on it in
  PR-2's review.
- **AM-3 (D-3, C5, C6, RULE-5 "walk", R-5, §5):** arcade PR-4 has landed. C5's serialisation is moot.
  `DevelopHand.next` already separates the source from two runners, `tick` and `steer`, so C6 adds a second source and
  both runners play it. The development draft sizes its route with the floor's walk estimate (`floorDevWalk`) when the
  game steers. The serialised-hands row in §9 is void; the PM already ruled on 29 Oct that step-2 PR-3 rebases onto
  arcade PR-4.
- **AM-4 (D-4, C11):** the AI steers through `AiSeat.developSteer → FloorInput`, never through an `AiAction`. `steer`
  and `act` therefore become permanent exclusions, with the reason "the AI plays them through FloorInput". The
  exclusion's "until arcade PR-4" end date is dropped.
- **AM-5 (D-5, C7, RULE-10):** the auction hand now trades at the store's and the ship's line (`lineTrade`). On the
  playbook path the line trade stays as today, because playbook v1 has no market play (P17).
- **AM-6 (C4, §5, T-AC-12):** the "table lines" clause of C4 belongs to the development and auction draft points, so it
  moves from PR-1 to PR-3. The planning draft point adds no line, and the land line is still said at the grant.
- **AM-7 (P12, found by T-AC-3 while building, 4 Nov):** P12's "a seller of Food or Energy holds at least next month's
  need (`monthNeeds`)" contradicts RULE-5, under which the draft holds what today's trade pick holds. Today's pick sells
  Energy below the gross `monthNeeds` when the seat's own plants will replace it, and the property test caught 21 cases
  on seeds 1–3. PR-1 reads P12's floor as the hands' own `unitValues(…).floorUnits`, the measure RULE-10 and C7 already
  use ("keep ≥ floorUnits"), so INV-1 holds and the playbook seat plays as today. A profile with no level keeps
  `monthNeeds`. P12's text in §2 is to be updated to match; WS1 to review.
- **AM-8 (§3 row 2):** `AiSeat.draft(state, phase, month = state.month)`. The optional `month` serves AM-1's month stamp
  and its pending-pick rule. Existing callers are unaffected.
