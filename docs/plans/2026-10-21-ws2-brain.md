| Goal | Who benefits | Value delivered | Proof | First delivered in |

mule-game.ts, which other lanes own and the PM cleared for PR-3; and the latency spike (run by Heiko after PR-2) can

command and a measured cost per call.

**Non-goals (spec §0), lifted verbatim. A task serving one is cut:**
- lobby Smart/Classic picker, credit packs and pricing, cost dashboard, monthly account and service caps with alerts,
  memory and grudges, whispers, deal negotiation, the model proposing its own variants, bring-your-own-key, other
  providers, MCP seats, a new chat filter (AI lines pass the room's existing `moderate`), balance tuning (WS1).

**Thin baseline (spec §0.1):** decider only, lines kept in the decision log · 1 PR, about 1 day · loses because
`GameContext` has no chat method, so no line reaches the table and "chats in character" fails (checked again at plan
time: `packages/tabletop/src/room.ts::GameContext` still has `now`, `schedule`, `changed` only).
→ **PR-1 relationship:** the baseline lost for a true reason; PR-1 is the pure core of the chosen approach, the first
step of the shortest path to the table.

**Constraints:** spec §0.2 (HC-1 … HC-6) travel with every task.

**Architecture:** a pure LLM decider in `packages/ai/src/llm/` talks to the model only through a port; the server's
`apps/server/src/brain/` fills the port with a Haiku client that logs every call to a JSONL ledger and refuses calls
past a seat's budget; MuleGame passes each AI seat its decider and routes chat both ways through two optional
room hooks.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript (ESM), pnpm workspaces, vitest, `@anthropic-ai/sdk` ^0.137.0 (server only), Node `fs`.
