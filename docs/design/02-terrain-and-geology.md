# Terrain and geology

What you see is not what you get. Every plot shows its terrain, but terrain only tells you the odds. What a plot can really produce is hidden in the ground, and only a geo analysis tells you. Two hills side by side can be a rich Smithore seam and an empty rock.

## Two layers

- **Terrain** is visible to everyone. It sets the odds for each good and the event risks (floods, quakes, dust storms).
- **Ground** is hidden. When the map is made, every plot rolls its real rating for each good from its terrain's odds. Production is base output plus the ground rating, plus adjacency and upgrades as before.

The ground rating replaces the fixed terrain bonuses ("River: Food +2") and the yield traits (fertile soil, rich vein, underground spring, magnetic anomaly). The hazard traits stay: bedrock, sheltered, hot spring, thin vein, toxic soil, unstable ground, infested and flood plain.

## Six raw goods

Rare earth joins as the sixth raw good.

- **Grown goods** (Food, Water, Energy) depend on soil, water table, sun, wind and heat. Rated from -2 to +3. A -2 plot still works, just badly.
- **Mined goods** (Smithore, Crystite, Rare earth) are deposits, rated 0 to 4. A 0 means there is nothing to mine, so a MULE outfitted for it produces nothing.
- **Rare earth** is what Electronics needs. Decided by Heiko on 21 Oct 2026: Electronics is made from Rare earth + Alloy + Energy, and Crystite goes back to being the pure off-world luxury, as in the original. Crystite stays the speculator's good; Rare earth becomes the industry bottleneck next to Alloy.

## Terrain odds

"Often good" means most plots of that terrain roll a bonus, not all of them. On hills, for example, about seven in ten plots have Smithore 2 or more, but one in ten has none at all.

| Terrain | Often good for | Often poor for | Mineral odds (Smithore / Crystite / Rare earth) | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| River | Food, Water | Mining | low / none / low | Floods |
| Lake | Water, Food | Mining | none / none / none | Floods |
| Plains | Food, Energy (wind) | Nothing | low / low / low | Dust storms |
| Forest | Food, Medicine factories | Energy | low / none / low | Wildfire |
| Swamp | Water, Medicine factories | Food | low / low / medium | Disease |
| Hills | Smithore | Food | high / medium / medium | Mild quakes |
| Mountains | Smithore | Food, Energy | very high / medium / high | Quakes, rockslides |
| Desert | Energy (solar) | Water, Food | low / medium / medium | Dust storms, drought |
| Tundra (new) | Energy (wind), Water | Food | medium / low / medium | Cold snaps |
| Ice field | Water | Food, Energy | none / low / low | Cold snaps |
| Volcanic | Energy (geothermal) | Food | medium / low / high | Eruptions |
| Crater | Crystite | Food | low / very high / medium | Meteor strikes |

Wasteland is dropped; tundra takes its place. Special fields and super tiles sit on top of this as before. A super tile is always a 4 in its good and is only ever found by a geo analysis.

## Geo analysis

One action, one answer. This replaces the three survey levels (quick look, survey, deep survey).

- **On site:** during development a colonist stands on any plot, owned or free, for about 8 seconds and gets its full ground card: every good's rating and any hazard traits.
- **From orbit:** in the planning phase you can order a geo drone for a fee to analyse up to two free plots anywhere. The result arrives at the start of the land grant, in time to pick.
- **Private:** the card shows only on your own map. You can share it, sell it in a deal card, or lie about it in chat. The fake assay sabotage still plants false cards.
- **Signals:** an analysis next to a super tile reports a strong signal nearby, without saying where.

Before an analysis your map shows the terrain odds as faint hints. After it, the real numbers replace them.

## On the map

- The land is drawn as one continuous landscape: rivers flow and bend, lakes have shores, mountains have relief and snow, deserts have dunes, ice glints. Plots are a fine holo grid laid over it, not coloured squares.
- Analysed plots show small glyphs for their best goods with 1 to 4 pips. Smithore, Crystite and Rare earth each have their own glyph, so a rich crater reads at a glance.
- Owned plots glow in the owner's colour and shape, as before.
