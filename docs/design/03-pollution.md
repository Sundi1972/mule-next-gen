# Pollution

Industry makes money, but it fouls the land, the water and the air. Pollution turns the colony's health into something everyone shares: one player can get rich from a dirty mine while the whole table pays for it. That makes it a natural subject for deals, votes and arguments. All numbers here are placeholders until the balance sim has run.

## Two levels of pollution

**On each plot (local).** Every plot has a pollution level from 0 to 3: clean, dusty, polluted, toxic. A polluter raises its own plot and the four plots touching it by 1 each month, up to 3, whoever owns them. A plot with no polluter next to it recovers by 1 a month on its own.

**In the colony (air).** The colony has one pollution gauge from 0 to 100, shown on the big screen and every phone. Each polluter adds points every month; nature takes 3 points away every month by itself.

| Source | Colony points a month | Local spread |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rare earth mine | 4 | Yes |
| Alloy smelter | 3 | Yes |
| Electronics works | 1 | No |
| Smithore and Crystite mines | 1 | No |
| Farms, wells, Energy plants, Medicine labs | 0 | No |

## What pollution does

**On a plot.** Food, Water and Medicine made on a plot lose 1 per pollution level (a toxic plot loses 3). Mines, smelters and Electronics works don't mind.

**In the colony.** The gauge has four bands:

| Band | Gauge | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clean | 0 to 29 | Off-world buyers pay 10% more for exports from a clean colony. |
| Smoggy | 30 to 59 | Colonists get sick: everyone loses 5 seconds of development time, and colonists' Medicine demand rises by half. |
| Toxic | 60 to 79 | Everyone loses 10 seconds of development time; every farm and well makes 1 less; the epidemic event becomes more likely. |
| Collapse | 80 to 100 | Everyone loses 15 seconds; colonists start leaving, so colony demand for goods falls; the colony's final rating drops. |

**Medicine heals.** A player who has Medicine in stock uses 1 at the start of development and ignores the sickness time loss that month. That gives Medicine labs a real job in a dirty colony.

**The colony as a whole.** As in the original M.U.L.E., the game ends with a colony rating as well as a winner. Pollution at the end lowers that rating. If the colony ends in Collapse, the federation judges it a failure: the winner is still named, but everyone's final score is cut by a fifth. So even the player who polluted most has a reason not to let it get out of hand.

## Fighting pollution

**Upgrades on plots and factories** (built during development, like other upgrades):

- **Filter.** Paid in Alloy and fitted to a mine or factory. It halves the colony points and stops the local spread.
- **Green belt.** A cheap MULE outfit from the store that turns a plot into planted forest. It makes nothing, but it cleans its own plot and the four touching it by 1 a month and takes 2 points off the gauge.
- **Wild land.** Untouched forest and swamp plots clean themselves twice as fast, so leaving them wild has value.

**Colony projects** (the Governor spends the colony fund):

- **Cleanup squad.** Costs money every month it runs. It takes 8 points off the gauge and cleans the three most polluted plots by 1 level.
- **Air purifier tower.** One-off build paid in money and Alloy. It takes 3 points off the gauge every month for the rest of the game.

**Laws** the Governor can propose:

- **Pollution tax.** Each polluter pays per colony point it causes, into the colony fund that pays for cleanup.
- **Emission cap.** No new Rare earth mines or smelters while the gauge is above a level the proposer picks.
- **Green zone.** No polluters on river and lake plots or next to them.

**Between players.** A neighbour can be paid off with a deal card, or asked to fit a filter as part of a deal. Sabotage can also be a toxic spill, dumped on a rival's land.

## On screen

- The big screen shows the colony gauge with its band, next to the month counter, and a light haze over polluted plots on the map.
- The phone's plot card shows the plot's pollution level and what it costs that plot.
- At month end, the results screen says how much pollution each player caused, so everyone can see who is dirtying the colony.

## Events

Pollution feeds the event catalogue: acid rain (farms lose output in the most polluted area), toxic spill (a mine leaks and pollutes a wider area), epidemic (more likely when the air is toxic; Medicine prices jump), and a federation inspection (fines the biggest polluter when the colony is above Smoggy).
