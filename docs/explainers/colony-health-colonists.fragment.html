Colony health: the colonists

What you'll see at the table

  • A health gauge beside the mood: the share of colonists who are well, from 100 down to 60. It never goes lower: at most 4 in 10 colonists are ever sick.
  • Hunger, thirst and missing Medicine make colonists sick, and sickness spreads. A few fall sick every month anyway; more when they went short; more again the more are already sick.
  • Fed and treated, they recover. About half the sick get well each month, fewer when Medicine is short. A colony that is fed and has its Medicine settles at about 3 sick in 100.
  • Sick colonists need more Medicine (up to 40% more), cost the mood up to 4 points of its aim, and slow the colony's growth by up to 2% a month.
  • Electronics are a comfort, never a need. When the store can give the colonists their Electronics (1 per 50 colonists a month), their mood aims up to 4 points higher. Missing them costs nothing.

How it works

Each month, after the colonists have eaten: new sick = the well share × (1 in 100 + up to 2 in 10 for hunger or thirst + up to 2 in 10 for missing Medicine + 15% of the share already sick). Recovered = half the sick share, cut by up to a third when Medicine is short. The sick share is capped at 4 in 10. Hunger and missing Medicine go through the same tolerance curve as the mood: a tenth short costs nothing.

Why it cannot spiral: contagion (15%) is far below recovery (50%), so whenever the colony is fed and treated, the sick share falls back toward 3 in 100 whatever it was. The ceiling only bounds the worst famine. The first version measured, with contagion equal to recovery, settled at 18 sick in 100 even when fully fed and sat at the ceiling to the end in 4 of 10 games; this one does not.

Sickness stays high only where the shortages last: Medicine was short in 64 of 120 months. In the three games whose shortages eased, the sick share fell from the ceiling to under 10 in 100 within 4 months. Health costs the mood and the colony almost nothing beyond the hunger itself.

Electronics today: the store stocked Electronics for the colonists in 1 month of 120, because nobody makes them; the bonus averaged +0.02. Adding the colonists' Electronics to the store's demand made it stock them, but the price ran to 80,738 credits with no maker.

Decisions for you

1 · Sickness with a ceiling and recovery stronger than contagion

  • As above: at most 4 in 10 sick; contagion 15%, recovery 50%. For: measured, it never runs away; sickness follows the shortages and clears when they clear. Against: six numbers to tune.
  • Stronger contagion (as strong as recovery). Against: measured, it never clears: 18 in 100 sick even when fully fed, and stuck at the ceiling in 4 games.

Recommended: as above.

2 · How much health weighs on the mood

  • Up to 4 points of the mood's aim at the ceiling, no sudden blow. For: the hunger already lands its blow; sickness is the slow after-effect. Measured: no worse than without health. Against: a sick colony is not much unhappier than a hungry one.
  • Up to 8 points. Against: measured in the first version, together with strong contagion, it pushed one more game into Unrest and cost 14 colonists a game.

Recommended: up to 4.

3 · The Electronics bonus: a comfort, sized below the hunger

  • Up to +4 to the mood's aim when met, never a penalty. About what one month at half rations costs, so comfort never outweighs hunger. For: it gives a reason to make Electronics beyond the colony's buildings. Against: today it almost never applies, because nobody makes Electronics.
  • Whether the store asks for the colonists' Electronics is the store's side (WS5): asking makes it stock them, but with no maker the price runs away (80,738 measured).

Recommended: +4, never a penalty. Whether the store asks for it goes with WS5's pricing ceiling.
