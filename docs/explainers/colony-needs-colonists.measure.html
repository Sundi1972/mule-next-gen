  • No rule ever reaches Content or Thriving. The colony starts at 60, right on Content's line, and something is always short.
  • Today's colony is underfed. Colonists went short in 91 of 120 months, and were more than half short in 79. The Water shelf was empty in 54. Wages alone made them go short in only 30 months, during price spikes late in the game. The shortage is supply, which is the consumption and demand part of this package.
  • The first numbers spiralled. Unhappy colonists strike and steal MULEs about three and a half times as often, which cuts supply further, and 8 games of 10 ended in Unrest. The softer numbers hold: 3 games of 10 touch Unrest, and trouble comes about one and a half times as often as today.
  • The thin option looks gentler here, and for the reason it loses. It caps every shortfall at 5 points a month, so a colony more than half short pays what it pays today. That is the flat cliff you asked to remove.
  • Letting the store restock without the colonists' wage limit is worth about 12 colonists by itself. The store keeps buying Food and Water when prices spike, instead of stopping at what the colonists could pay.
  • The store's lost income was 51,951 credits over the ten games. Its hidden running total was already below zero in 7 of them, and nothing reads it, so nothing needs replacing.
