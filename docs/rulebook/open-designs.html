Is there enough Food in the economy? Being designed

Once computer players buy the Food they need, they empty the same store shelf the colonists buy from. With the buying fix (Heiko's 'sell surplus', the computer players also keeping back the Energy and, with a farm, the Water they need), colonists are short of Food or Water in about 66-70% of months, against 62-66% if they kept back only their Food and about 50% before. The levers are Heiko's: start Food, the store's Food per player, Food spoilage (50%), farm yield.

Smarter buying and selling for computer players Designed

One value for every unit a computer player could hold: the chance it is needed times the cost of going short, plus the chance it is not times what it fetches later. Computer players buy their shortfall first, stock up when goods are cheap, sell surplus when dear, value power plants and wells at what they save, and later speculate. Claude's plan can set its price view.

Responsible: AI Strategy team (WS9), with the Balance team (WS1) for its files · Status: Heiko chose option B and said 'Yes, build it' (5 Nov); spec #385; the first piece ships switched off until measured · The rule in full: computer players

The store as a market maker Designed

Steadier prices (at most +30% / -25% a month), a ceiling of 12 times the usual price, sold-out bidding and gluts feed next month's price, the shelf moves the price before it empties, a 15% spread each side widening to 25% when short, Energy stocked for working MULEs, and moving off-world prices: Crystite typically 90 within 40-300, other exports half that swing. The MULE price gets the ceiling too.

Responsible: Maps team (WS5), with WS1 and WS3 for their files · Status: Heiko chose option B, the 12x ceiling and Crystite as proposed (5 Nov); spec #390, waiting for Heiko to read it · The rule in full: store and prices

Auction rules: stop at your need, switch sides, rest zones, bidding above a sold-out store Designed

Selling stops by itself at next month's need, whoever you sell to; holding the arrow for one second sells below it on purpose. Buyers get no automatic stop. You can switch between buying and selling during the walk. Rest bands above and below the store's lines stop accidental trades. When the store is sold out, buyers can walk above it and push the price up.

Responsible: Auctions team (WS3), screens by the UX team (WS6) · Status: Heiko: hold the arrow 1 s; the stop covers all sales; no buyer stop (5 Nov). Rules amendment a33879c4 · The rule in full: goods auction

Water is a base resource: every plot uses it Designed
