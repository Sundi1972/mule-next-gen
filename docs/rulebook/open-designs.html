Is there enough Food in the economy? Being designed

Once computer players buy the Food they need, they empty the same store shelf the colonists buy from. With the buying fix (Heiko's 'sell surplus', the computer players also keeping back the Energy and, with a farm, the Water they need), colonists are short of Food or Water in about 66-70% of months, against 62-66% if they kept back only their Food and about 50% before. The levers are Heiko's: start Food, the store's Food per player, Food spoilage (50%), farm yield.

Smarter buying and selling for computer players Designed

One value for every unit a computer player could hold: the chance it is needed times the cost of going short, plus the chance it is not times what it fetches later. Computer players buy their shortfall first, stock up when goods are cheap, sell surplus when dear, value power plants and wells at what they save, and later speculate. Claude's plan can set its price view.

Responsible: AI Strategy team (WS9), with the Balance team (WS1) for its files · Status: Heiko chose option B and said 'Yes, build it' (5 Nov); spec #385; the first piece ships switched off until measured · The rule in full: computer players

The store's prices on Heiko's five rules Designed

The store prices each good from the colony's months of cover: normal while the colony holds two months or makes what it eats, then tight, shortage and severe (1.25, 2 and 4 times normal) while it eats into its stock; colonists short last month counts as severe. Each step has its own spread. Prices go straight up, come down a quarter a month, never below normal, move halfway towards a high trade between players, stop at 12 times normal, and read the same on the hub, the auction and the TV. Food: 30, 38, 60, 120. Replaces the 5 Nov market-maker steps; the ceiling, sell-out rule, moving ship prices and MULE cap stay.

Responsible: Maps team (WS5), with WS1 and WS3 for their files · Status: Designed, not in the game yet, WS5 (Heiko approved the colony economy, 6 Nov); ships with the supply pass (decision 1) · The rule in full: store and prices

Medicine and Electronics demand Designed

Colonists take their Medicine from the store, free, and it counts as demand; short of it they fall sick. They want Electronics (a mood bonus when met, nothing lost when not), and that want also counts as demand for Rare earth and Alloy. The price is the signal; the 12x ceiling is a safety rail. Nothing is imported: colony buildings get Alloy and Electronics only from what the colony made. Decisions 14 and 15 are superseded by the health rule (Heiko, 6 Nov): Medicine reaches the colonists' mood only through sickness, and hunger raises the Medicine need only through the sick share.

Responsible: Maps team (WS5); the mood bonus is the Politics team's (WS7) · Status: Designed, not in the game yet, WS5 (Heiko, 6 Nov); ships with the Medicine supply (decision 1) · The rule in full: store and prices

Hungry, thirsty and sick crews work slower Designed

A crew left short at the month end works slower next month on the colonists' tolerance curve: nothing under a tenth short, half output with nothing. The farm's dry halving goes. From a fifth of the colony sick, the same share of crews is sick; one Medicine a month cures a player's crews.

Auction rules: stop at your need, switch sides, rest zones, bidding above a sold-out store Designed

Selling stops by itself at next month's need, whoever you sell to; holding the arrow for one second sells below it on purpose. Buyers get no automatic stop. You can switch between buying and selling during the walk. Rest bands above and below the store's lines stop accidental trades. When the store is sold out, buyers can walk above it and push the price up.
