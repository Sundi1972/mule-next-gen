Smarter buying and selling for computer players Being designed

Computer players look at their whole need, the store's prices and what is available, value having enough and a surplus when it is cheap, and later speculate on prices. Goals: stay supplied (short while the store had stock and they could pay in under 5% of months), value a surplus when cheap, and in a second step speculate under a price belief shaped by personality and settable by Claude.

Auction rules: auto-stop, flip sides, rest zones, bidding above a sold-out store Being designed

A seller stops automatically at their own balance point and moves up into the store lane; selling into a shortage becomes a conscious choice. You can change between buying and selling during the auction. Rest zones above and below the store's lines stop you trading by accident. When the store has sold out, buyers can walk above it and push the price up.

Energy shortage turns plots off, biggest user first Being built

Energy plots use 1 Energy each from their own output. When you are short, the plot that uses most is turned off first, then the next, until the rest fit; a turned-off plot makes 20% of its output. Today a short plot makes half, in map order.
