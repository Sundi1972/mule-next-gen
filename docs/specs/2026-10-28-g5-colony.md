## §0 · Business Goals & Value

**G-5 · The colony is a shared project worth building: prestige objects, a colony mood that shapes
events, and a colony score at game end.** `[handoff]`

- *Who benefits:* every player at the table, as a colonist with a stake in the colony beside their
  own fortune; and Heiko, who wants the colony itself to be won or lost.
- *Today:* the colony fund only pays the two offices. It nets about 1,300-1,500 cr a game (sales tax
  about 2,100-2,400 minus office pay about 850; WS1 rows on ws7-politics, SEAMS 2026-10-28 line 349).
  Nothing can be built together, the colony has no mood, events never answer to how the colony is
  run, and the game ends on personal scores only (`packages/engine/src/game.ts::scoreParts`).
  verified_by: read (code), executed (WS1 sim rows).
- *Value:* a second thing to play for. Paying tax and donating buy something everyone sees; running
  the colony badly has visible consequences (strikes, theft, emigration); the end screen says how the
  colony did, which the online league can later rank.
- *Proof* `[handoff]`:
  - **UBER-AC-5** In the sim (AI only, politics on, all three seed sets) a typical colony builds
    4-5 prestige objects in a 12-month game, and all of WS1's checks pass.
  - **UBER-AC-6** Unhappy months draw at least 2× the strikes and thefts of thriving months, and a
    strike cuts the struck good's plots to 20% output.
  - **UBER-AC-7** The game-over screen shows the colony score (wealth + environment + prestige, × a
    happiness factor of about 0.8-1.2) and each player's standing, and a replay of the seed and the
    moves reproduces it.

**Non-goals** (Heiko, 2026-10-28): the online league; spending prestige (the pot only grows);
free-text projects; Beginner difficulty changes (Beginner plays without mood or prestige).
Also not here: design 05's protective colony projects and the four other budget lines (Reserve,
Security, Environment, Welfare) stay in politics slice 5; the overrule vote and inquiry too.

**Rough cost:** chosen approach about 16 PRs in 6 lanes over 3 build waves after a design wave;
thin baseline about 3 PRs (WS7 2, WS3 1).

## §1 · Solution Design, by owner `[handoff]`

The colony gets one engine module, `packages/engine/src/colony.ts` (new, WS7), holding the fund's
ledger, the project pipeline, donations and the happiness gauge. Everything else plugs into
existing owners' seams.

*Reuse check (self-review 6).* `grep -rni "project|happiness|prestige|donat|mood"` over
packages/engine/src and packages/ai/src finds only the Treasury doc comment ("filled only by taxes,
fines and donations"); no project, mood or donation code exists. The fund has three writers on
trunk 9a409a56 (`git grep -n "treasury.money" packages apps`, non-test, writes only):
`economy.ts::taxSale` (sales tax in), `politics.ts::payOffices` (pay out) and
`events.ts::applyEvents`'s `federationTax` case (the event's levy in; AM-2). C20 routes all three
through colony.ts. colony.ts is new because politics.ts is elections and offices (its header); the
fund and projects are the colony's, read by politics, not owned by it.

inputs    source ∈ salesTax | federationTax | exportTax | landTax | pollutionTax | donation | grant
          line   ∈ officePay | project | upkeep | repair | strikePay | siteBid

  #   call                       then
  1   fundIn(source, amount)     money += amount; colony.ledger.in[source] += amount

RULE-20 · Happiness drift (C23)                                      shape: formula

evaluated at  upkeep, at the end of runColony (after the objects' upkeep, building and funding) and
              before the month-end snapshot, so the snapshot's happiness is this month's. RULE-27's
              upkeep order is unchanged: runColony is still its last step.
inputs        short     the goods short at this upkeep: runUpkeep's shortages, which game.ts passes in
                        as runColony(state, shortages) (WS3 named edit, one line). Not the report's:
                        game.ts writes report.shortages only after runColony returns  0..GOODS.length
              air       state.air, the colony's air gauge: only production moves it, so at upkeep it is
                        the month's report.air                                0..100, higher is dirtier
              tax       the sales tax in whole %                              POLITICS.tax.min..max
              broken    1 if colony.promises holds this month's entry with kept false, else 0
              prestige  colonyPrestige(state): standing objects + permanent    ≥ 0
              gap       richest − poorest player by cash + land (GAME.plotScore a plot), one helper
              welfare   0 until slice 5 (D-G5-5): no weight, no input
              h         colony.happiness, a whole number 0..100, HAPPINESS.start when the game starts

formula       H = HAPPINESS, w = H.weights
  target = H.base − w.shortage × short − w.air × max(0, air − w.airFree) − w.tax × max(0, tax − w.taxFree)
                  − w.broken × broken + w.prestige × min(prestige, w.prestigeCap)
                  − w.gap × min(gap / w.gapUnit, w.gapCap)
  target = clamp(target, 0, 100)
  h'     = clamp(Math.round(h + clamp(target − h, −H.maxStep, +H.maxStep)), 0, 100)

nudges        moodShift(state, delta): an event's direct change (the festival's "Mood +10"), applied at
              once, clamped 0..100 and NOT capped by maxStep. It is the only other writer of h; WS1's
              events (C26) call it, so colony.ts stays the sole owner of colony.happiness.
band          moodBand(h): h ≥ H.bands.thriving Thriving · ≥ content Content · ≥ restless Restless ·
              ≥ unhappy Unhappy · else Unrest (placeholders 80 / 60 / 40 / 20, C23)
unknown       no Governor or no promise this month → broken 0 · one player → gap 0 · nothing short →
              short 0. Nothing unknown moves the mood.
boundaries    every number is a named HAPPINESS value, tuned at the end (HC-25). Politics off → no colony,
              so no happiness.
examples      placeholders: base 60, maxStep 5, shortage 8 a good, air 0.4 a point over 30, tax 2 a point
              over 5, broken 10, prestige 2 a point up to 15, gap 2 per 1,000 cr up to 10
              h 60, a quiet month (nothing short, air 20, tax 5, nothing standing, gap 0) → target 60 → h 60
              h 60, two goods short, air 70 → target 60 − 16 − 16 = 28 → h 55, Restless
              h 30, quiet months with a park standing (prestige 2) → target 64 → 35, 40 … 60 after 6 months
              h 78, a festival's moodShift(+10) → 88, Thriving, whatever the month's step

RULE-25 · The colony score and each player's standing (C31, C32, C34)   shape: formula

## §7 · Risks & Spikes `[review-only]`

- **Income gap.** The fund nets 1,300-1,500 cr a game; 4-5 objects at the placeholder prices
  (small 300-500, medium 1,050-1,250, large 2,450-3,400) plus about 5% upkeep need several times
  that. Mitigation: wave 0's affordability study and D-G5-2 before any build (HC-25).
- **Mood runaway.** Unhappy → strikes → shortages → unhappier. Mitigation: RULE-20's capped monthly
  step and a festival/settler-boom side in C26; a T-AC that a colony recovers from 30 within N good
  months.
- **Event-model dependency.** C26 waits for WS1's redesign. Mitigation: C23 ships first and is read
  through a fixed stub in tests.
- **Prestige that can fall.** The catalogue's riot damages an object ("no prestige until
  repaired"), so prestige is the working objects' sum plus permanent points, not a pot that only
  grows (HC-23 reworded). Mitigation: the end score reads the final state, and RULE-21 makes repair
  a fund spend the Governor can choose.
- **Replay not recorded today.** UBER-AC-7 needs C32. Mitigation: C32 in wave 1.
- **Beginner and politics-off drift.** Mitigation: HC-22 and WS1's byte-identical off rows on every
  money-moving PR.
