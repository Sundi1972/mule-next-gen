## §0 · Business Goals & Value

### MG-1 · Every map is playable and fair `[handoff]`

- **Who benefits** `[review-only]`: every player at the table, the family and the AI seats alike.
- **Today** `[review-only]`: one four-player map in seven has under one good Smithore plot (deposit 3+) per player, and
  on those the corral sells out in 81% of games (mean 3.4 months, Smithore peaking near 966 against a reference of 50);
  at three or more per player it is 30%. 8% of four-player maps have no hills or mountains. From the worst plot, good
  Smithore is a median 9 steps away, and some maps have none. `verified_by: executed` (300 four-player sim games and
  500 maps per size on e4785fc; `.handover/ws5-findings/floors-measure.ts`, `shape-measure.ts`).
- **Value** `[review-only]`: no game is lost to the deal of the map, and G-1's balance checks stop swinging on map
  luck, so WS1's numbers measure the economy, not the dice.
- **UBER-AC-1:** `pnpm --filter @mule/engine test map-quality` builds 1,000 maps for each player count from 2 to 6 through
  `createGame`, and every one meets every floor and reach rule. It runs in CI, and goes red when a rule is broken on purpose.

### MG-2 · Every map is a varied, lively planet that reads well at the table `[handoff]`

- **Who benefits** `[review-only]`: the family, for whom the landscape is the board; Heiko, judging it by eye.
- **Today** `[review-only]`: every map has a straight river down the middle column; 7% of plots sit in one- or two-plot
  specks; one terrain covers up to 56% of a map; a three-player map is usually missing five of the twelve terrains;
  there are no special fields and no deliberate scarcity. `verified_by: executed` (same scripts).
- **Value** `[review-only]`: each game starts differently and readably; special fields and the occasional scarce good
  give players something to fight over at the land auction (G-3).
- **UBER-AC-2:** Heiko approves the map gallery (many seeds, current vs new, each with its numbers), and the same CI
  test enforces every shape rule (RULE-1 rows S1-S9) and the special-field rules (RULE-5).

**Non-goals** `[review-only]`
- Super tiles: only their rule is reserved (RULE-1 row X1). Built later, with politics and their counters.
- Unexplored map edges, roads, outposts, and the other five special fields (outpost, spaceport pad, federation beacon,
  old road, creature lair).
- The classic 9×5 map; map styles (archipelago and so on).
- Fixing colonist hunger: it does not track map ground (about 90% of games are hungry whatever the map). WS1's item.
- Changing the 10 balance checks, or tuning any non-map number.
- A river that forks or loops back (needs a renderer change in WS4's package).

**Rough cost** `[review-only]`: chosen approach A: 4 PRs into ws5-maps, about 2-3 days of agent time; the generator PR
is the largest. Thin baseline B: about half the code.

## §0.1 · Decision Record `[review-only]`

- **Chosen, A: build the map by construction.** River walk, budgeted terrain shares, spread region seeds grown together,
  ground dealt from each terrain's exact odds along a smooth field, then floors, reach and seams fixed by swapping
  ratings between plots of the same terrain; scarcity; special fields. One pure rules checker serves the CI test and the
  gallery. A throwaway prototype (`.handover/ws5-findings/spike-generator.ts`) met floors, reach, river, scarcity rate
  and hint honesty on 1,500 maps; the existing test suite passed on its maps; region layout is the open engineering.
- **Thin baseline, B: patch today's nearest-seed generator** (bend the river, merge specks, lift ground until floors and
  reach pass). About half the code. **Loses because:** today's layout leaves hills and mountains off 8% of four-player
  maps (measured, `shape-measure.ts`), so the Smithore floor could only be met by lifting plains or forest to deposit 3,
  which `TERRAIN_ODDS` says can't happen there (their best is 2). That breaks the hint-honesty rule S9. Checkable:
  `npx tsx .handover/ws5-findings/maps-baseline.ts` from packages/engine prints the no-hills share.
- **Rejected, C: A plus a map-style system.** Outside MG-1/MG-2 (non-goal).
- **Rejected inside A: reroll until the rules pass.** Ruled out by the spawn document; also unbounded in time.

## §2 · The Logic `[handoff]`

### RULE-1 · What a good map is (the checker's table)    shape: decision table (each row a pass condition)

Terms: *land* = every plot but the store; *dry land* = land that is not river or lake. A plot is *good* for Food, Water or
Energy at rating +1 or more, for Smithore, Crystite or Rare earth at 3 or more (`MAP.goodAt`). *Per player* = count on
the map ÷ players. *Steps* = Manhattan distance. Size bands: small = 13×7 (2-3 players), mid = 15×9 (4), large = 19×11 (5-6).

| # | Rule | Pass when | Numbers (MAP) |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | Floors | for Food, Water, Energy and Smithore, good plots per player ≥ floor (Crystite and Rare earth: their rings replace floors, AM-1) | food 10, water 10, energy 10, smithore 3 |
| F2 | One scarce good at most | goods below their normal range ≤ 1 | normal range lower edge: food 12, water 13, energy 13, smithore 4 |
| F3 | Reach | from every land plot, a good Food, Water and Energy plot within R steps, and a good Smithore plot within RS | R 3 (small, mid), 4 (large); RS 5 (small, mid), 6 (large) |
| S1 | All terrains | all 11 land terrains present (the river's presence is S6's) | — |
| S2 | Shares | each terrain's share of non-river land is within its target ± a third, ± 1 plot | plains 24, forest 11, hills 10, desert 9, tundra 8, mountains 7, swamp 7, lake 6, volcanic 6, crater 6, ice 6 (%) |
| S3 | No dominant terrain | no terrain over a third of non-river land | — |
| S4 | No specks | every connected same-terrain region of non-river land has ≥ 3 plots | minRegion 3 |
| S5 | No blob | no region larger than ceil(12% of non-river land) | maxRegionShare 0.12 |
| S6 | River shape | every row holds 1 river plot or 2 touching ones; rows 0 and height-1 hold river; some river plot in each row shares a column with a river plot of the row above, so the river is one continuous channel | — |
| S7 | River bends | rows holding two river plots (each is one bend) ≥ B; the river keeps ≥ 2 columns from each side; a river plot touches the store | B 2 (small), 3 (mid), 4 (large) |
| S8 | Smithore seams | ≥ 70% of good Smithore plots touch another good Smithore plot | seamShare 0.7 |
| S10 | Crystite and Rare earth rings (AM-1) | for each of the two: `peak.count` min to max plots are rated `peak.high`; every land plot's rating is fixed by its distance d to the nearest high plot: d=0 high, d=1 exactly `peak.medium`, d=2 within `peak.low`, d≥3 exactly 0 (so where rings overlap a plot keeps the highest value). No terrain or river-side condition | high 4, medium 3, low 1-2; count 3-4 per mineral, every map size |
| S9 | Honest hints (aggregate, CI only) | over 1,000 maps per size: for Food, Water, Energy and Smithore, each terrain's mean rating is within 0.3 of `expectedGround`; for Crystite and Rare earth, the mean over all land is within 0.3 of `MAP.unknownMineral`, the same hint on every terrain; and (AM-7) on each part of the map (each edge row and column, and the interior), the mean of rating minus hint for those four goods is within 0.10 of 0 | honesty 0.3; placeHonesty 0.10; unknownMineral = the per-map mean, each map counted once, over all sizes (measured 0.6) |
| P1 | Special fields | RULE-5 holds | see RULE-5 |
| X1 | Super tiles (reserved, not built) | when built: placed after F1-F2, never counted toward a floor or the scarcity band, spaced like special fields | 2 (small), 3 (mid, large) |

`unknown`: a map with no river plot fails S6 (it is not "a river of zero bends"). A map with no plot rated `peak.high`
for a mineral fails S10 (zero peaks is not "no ring broken").

examples → §6: a hand-built map with Smithore 2.75 per player fails F1 only · two goods below normal fails F2 ·
a corner plot 6 steps from good Smithore on a mid map fails F3 · a two-plot lake fails S4 · a river row with plots in
columns 5 and 7 fails S6 · today's generator on seed 1 (4 players) fails S7 (zero bends).

### RULE-2 · The generator pipeline    shape: sequence (each stage reads only the stages before it)

| # | Stage | What it does | Guarantees |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | River | Puts the store at the centre. The river plot in the store's row is one column left or right (by rng). Then it walks up and down, shifting one column on B to B+1 rows chosen by rng, never on the two rows touching the store's row, clamped to ≥ 2 columns from the sides. A shift adds the joining plot on the row nearer the store, at the column of the row further from it, so every row shares a column with the row above. | S6, S7 |
| 2 | Budget | Jitters each share by ± a third (rng), caps any share at 30%, turns shares into plot counts that sum to the free land, with every terrain ≥ minRegion. | S1, S2, S3 |
| 3 | Regions | (AM-4) Mining terrain (hills or mountains) is placed so every land plot has some within Smithore reach. Splits each terrain's count into regions of minRegion to maxRegionShare (about 6 plots each). Seeds each region on a free plot far from same-terrain seeds, then grows all regions together, a free neighbour at a time, weighted by quota left. A region never takes a plot touching another region of its own terrain. Pockets that boxed-in regions leave get a new region of the terrain furthest under budget. Then a bounded local repair scores the layout on S1-S5 (and AM-4's coverage) and applies the best single move, else the best run of up to 3 moves found by a beam search under one evaluation budget (moves may carve a minRegion-sized region out of a big patch), else up to a fixed number of moves that leave it level and then a few that make it worse, skipping recently changed plots; it returns the best layout seen (AM-5). | S4, S5 (S1-S3 kept) |
| 4 | Ground | For Food, Water, Energy and Smithore, a smooth field (rng blobs: narrow for Smithore, wide for grown goods) ranks the plots, and each terrain's plots are dealt that terrain's ratings by stratified draw, highest rating to highest field. Crystite and Rare earth are 0 everywhere until stage 4b. | per-terrain ratings follow `TERRAIN_ODDS` (S9); Smithore clusters (S8, mostly) |
| 4b | Peaks (AM-1) | For Crystite, then Rare earth: draws 3 or 4 peak centres (rng) on random land plots, any terrain, rings free to overlap with each other and with the other mineral's. Each ring raises its plots to at least: centre `peak.high`, 4 orthogonal land neighbours `peak.medium`, land plots 2 steps away `peak.low` (1 or 2 by rng). | S10 |
| 5 | Scarcity pick | With chance `MAP.scarceChance` (0.25) one good, chosen equally from Food, Water, Energy and Smithore (AM-1), is the scarce good. Drawn here so the draw order never depends on later repairs. | RULE-3 |
| 6 | Floors | (As built: AM-6.) For each good, if good plots < target (the floor for the scarce good, the normal lower edge otherwise), lift the non-good plots of the best-suited terrains, highest field first, to the good threshold. | F1, F2 (lower side) |
| 7 | Reach | (As built: AM-6.) For Food, Water, Energy and Smithore: while a plot is beyond reach, pick a non-good plot near it in the best-suited terrain and swap its rating with a good plot of the same terrain whose loss strands nobody; if none exists, lift it. Bounded passes. | F3 |
| 8 | Seams | (As built: AM-6.) For Smithore: an isolated good plot swaps ratings with a plot of the same terrain that touches another good plot, if that strands nobody. | S8 |
| 9 | Scarcity apply | (As built: AM-6.) Lowers the scarce good's good plots, lowest field first, to just under the threshold until the count is under the normal lower edge, skipping any whose loss would break reach. | F2 |
| 10 | Traits, peaks | As today: 0-2 hazard traits per plot, 1-3 peaks on mountains. | unchanged |
| 11 | Special fields | RULE-5. | P1 |

Invariant: stages 7-8 swap ratings only between plots of the same terrain, so each terrain's multiset of ratings is
unchanged by them, except their recorded lifts (stage 7's reach lifts, stage 8's seam lifts). Stages 4b, 6, 7-8's lifts and 9 change a terrain's ratings; a
reach lift goes first on a terrain whose odds allow a good rating, and a lift on a terrain whose odds forbid it is counted. S9 bounds their total drift, measured with the rings in place; for Crystite and Rare earth against the one map-wide hint (AM-1).
Invariant: every loop has a fixed pass limit (HC-3). If a repair stage cannot satisfy its rule within its passes, the map
is still returned; the CI test is what catches it.

### RULE-3 · The scarcity twist    shape: decision table

inputs: `scarceDraw` (a number in [0,1) from the rng at stage 5) · `pick` (one of Food, Water, Energy, Smithore)

| # | when | then |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | scarceDraw < MAP.scarceChance | `pick` (Food, Water, Energy or Smithore) is scarce: its good plots land in [floor × players, normal lower edge × players) |
| 2 | otherwise | no good is scarce; every good is at or above its normal lower edge |

outcome: a replay from the same seed makes the same map, with the same scarce good or none.
examples → §6: over 1,000 four-player maps, 200-300 have exactly one scarce good and none has two.

### RULE-4 · Reach and seams never fight    shape: invariant

A swap or a lowering is skipped when it would leave any land plot beyond reach (F3) of that good. Seams (stage 8) and
scarcity (stage 9) therefore can't undo reach (stage 7).

### RULE-5 · Special-field placement    shape: decision table (a candidate plot must pass every row)

Count by size: 3 (small), 4 (mid), 5 (large) (`MAP.specials`). Kinds drawn by rng with no kind more than twice and at
least 3 kinds on the map; if no plot fits a drawn kind, another kind is drawn (bounded).

| # | Kind | Allowed on | Extra condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ruins | dry land | — |
| 2 | Crystal cavern | crater, mountains, hills | the plot's Crystite ground ≥ 1 |
| 3 | Geyser | volcanic, tundra, ice | — |
| 4 | Oasis | desert | ≥ 2 of its 4 neighbours are desert |
| 5 | Alien relic | dry land | — (AM-8: the store row below already keeps it ≥ 2 steps away) |
| all | — | never river, lake, the store or a plot touching the store | ≥ 4 steps from every other special field; at least one special field on each side of the river |

Special fields don't count toward any floor: F1-F3 and S10 are checked on ground alone, before stage 11 and after it.
`SpecialField = { kind: 'ruins' | 'crystalCavern' | 'geyser' | 'oasis' | 'relic'; salvage?: 'alloy' | 'electronics' }`;
the ruins' salvage good is drawn at placement.

### RULE-6 · Special-field effects    shape: decision table (numbers in MAP.specialEffect)

## §7 · Risks & Spikes `[review-only]`

- **Region layout is unproven.** The prototype still breaks S2, S4 and S5 on 10-35% of maps. Mitigation: PR-2's plan
  begins with the regions stage under its own unit tests (T-AC-5), checked on 1,000 maps before the ground stages are
  built on it. Stop condition: if S4/S5 can't reach zero failures in one day, take relaxed numbers (minRegion 3 kept,
  maxRegionShare 0.15) to the PM with the measured failure rates.
- **Peaks are unprototyped (AM-1).** The prototype predates the ring rule. Mitigation: stage 4b gets its own stage test
  (T-AC-5); it is simple (random centres, max-of-rings), so the risk is in its knock-ons, not the stage.
- **Ring density varies by map size (AM-1).** 3-4 rings on every size: about 15-20 good plots per mineral, so about 7-10
  per player on a 2-player map and 3 on a 6-player map; the map-wide average (the hint) moves from about 0.85 (13×7) to
  0.4 (19×11). Mitigation: one hint for all sizes stays within S9's 0.3 if the measured means are as estimated; checked in
  PR-2's test. If not, take a per-size ring count to the PM.
- **Caverns need Crystite.** RULE-5 puts a crystal cavern only on crater, mountains or hills with Crystite ≥ 1, i.e. where
  a ring crosses those terrains. Mitigation: the kind is redrawn when no plot fits (bounded); the gallery shows how often.
- **Sim numbers move.** Measured early with the prototype's maps (old hints): corral sold out 11% → 6% (seeds 1-40) and
  10% → 7% (101-140); MULE price 90th percentile 406 → 252 and 312 → 280; Food-above-3× 50% → 53% on seeds 1-40 (one game
  over the 50% edge), 43% → 35% on 101-140; hunger unchanged. Mitigation: B-AC-8, with WS1 re-measuring G-1 after milestone 1.
- **Ground that varies by place (found by B-AC-8, 2026-10-22; fixed, AM-7).** The first build's edge plots ran 0.2-0.5
  below their hint, and the AI, which claims in reading order, landed on them: hunger 34% → 48% and Food 3× ok → MISS.
  Mitigation: S9 now also checks each edge and the interior; the fields reach past the edges. Residual: the AI's
  reading-order claim is exposed to any place-varying ground (passed to WS1).
- **Map creation cost.** About 3-4 ms per 4-player map, against near-instant before; a test building 1,000 games
  needed a 30 s timeout (WS3, named edit). Backlog: under 1 ms.
- **Hints move (D2b).** AI valuations shift with expectedGround. Mitigation: B-AC-8's sim rows; WS1 told at milestone 1.
- **Test runtime.** 5,000 maps per run. Mitigation: T-AC-4; if over budget, 1,000 for 4 players and 400 for the others,
  raised to the PM.
- No spike needed beyond the one done.
