## §0 · Business Goals & Value `[handoff: goal lines and proofs]` `[review-only: who / today / value / cost]`

All three goals come from Heiko's brief of 2026-10-21 ("look at the auction… the two dashed lines… look at how the
store looks… you can actually walk in… the map and how the resources are shown… the goods fly to your warehouse") and
his rulings that day (D1, R1, events "cool visual representation on the map, nice animations"). They serve Mission
G-3 (a family plays a whole game at the kitchen table) and, through readability, G-1.

**G-1 · A family can read the auction at a glance: how far apart buyers and sellers are, and when a trade happens.**
- **Who benefits:** every player at the table, most of all the youngest and the new ones, and whoever watches the TV.
- **Today:** the board shows each creature's own ask or bid; to see whether a trade is near you compare heights by
  eye. Nobody else's units are on the board, the store's stock is nowhere on it, and each good spends 5 s in a
  separate "Buyer / Seller / Pass" stage after its 6 s status screen (`WALK.declareMs`, `WALK.statusMs`), up to nine
  goods a month. `verified_by: read` (WalkBoard.tsx, Auction.tsx, auction.ts, config.ts).
- **Value:** the moment the original was loved for, the two lines closing in, is back; players decide faster with the
  numbers in front of them; a month loses up to 5 s per good that comes up.
- **Proof, `UBER-AC-1`:** in a goods auction, phone and TV both show a red lowest-ask line and a green highest-bid line
  across the board with their prices on the axis, each creature carries its units and surplus, the store's stock is
  on its selling line, and the view never enters a stage called `declare` (sides are picked on the status screen).
  Checked by the e2e auction steps on `data-testid` `line-ask`, `line-bid`, `units-<id>` and the stages it sees.

**G-2 · Everyone sees what each plot made and why, and what the month's event did, on the map.**
- **Who benefits:** every player; the TV room as a whole when an event hits.
- **Today:** production lasts 5 s (`PHASE_MS.production`). The phone lists "+n" per good; the TV shows a month-end
  panel. The engine computes clusters, Energy and Water shortages, factory fit and pollution
  (`packages/engine/src/production.ts::runProduction`) but records only a total per player and good, so none of it is ever visible. No
  events exist in the engine yet (WS1 is building ten, approved 2026-10-21). `verified_by: read`.
- **Value:** players learn the economy from the map, as the concept's design rule demands ("visible on the map"); an
  event becomes a table moment everyone watches.
- **Proof, `UBER-AC-2`:** in a seeded game where an event fires, the TV's production phase shows bars on every
  working plot, a badge on each plot whose output differs from its ground, the event's own animation over the plots
  it hit, and the goods flying into each player's slot of a colony bar, before the auction starts. Checked by an e2e
  screenshot step on `map-show`, `plot-<x>-<y>`, `hit-<x>-<y>`, `colony-bar`.

**G-3 · Development feels like the original: you walk into the store, buy and outfit your MULE among the others, and
walking costs time.**
- **Who benefits:** every player; the TV watchers, who see the race for the last MULE.
- **Today:** the store is a grid of buttons (`Store.tsx::StoreInterior`); buy, outfit and install are instant taps;
  the only thing that costs development time is a geo analysis (8 s). So Food, which sets your time, buys almost
  nothing; where you build doesn't matter; the Zipwing's "moves twice as fast" perk (`SPECIES_PERK.zipwingSpeed`)
  is read by nothing. The TV shows no creatures during development. `verified_by: read`.
- **Value:** Food = time matters again, plots near the store are worth more, and the room watches everyone race.
- **Proof, `UBER-AC-3`:** once R1's numbers are approved: on the phone, tapping the corral walks your creature across
  the store floor and the MULE is bought on arrival, with the walk's time gone from your clock; on the TV every
  colonist walks the map; a seeded server test shows a Zipwing reaching a plot in half a Mollox's time.

**Non-goals** (each needs its own spec if wanted)
- The original's art, layout, names or trade dress (concepts only).
- The event engine itself: WS1 builds the ten events. This spec shows them.
- R1's numbers: WS1 measures walking speeds in the sim; Heiko approves them.
- The original's assay office and land office booths: our analysis happens on the plot and listings in planning.
- A joystick or free steering: tap to walk only.
- An always-visible colony bar and stock strip on every screen: the mocks show them in production. Making them
  permanent is WS4's shell decision, a follow-up.
- Sound for the show (the TV's sound is WS4's), the land grant, politics, special fields (WS5's Old road and Outpost
  wait for R1).

**Rough cost:** chosen approach about 8 PRs over four lanes (WS3 three, WS6 three, WS1 one, WS4 one), the last two
gated on R1. Thin baseline about 2 PRs (below).

### §0.1 · Decision Record `[review-only]`

- **Chosen:** the five approved mock sets, built in three waves: auction readability (no rule change beyond R2), the
  production show (needs WS1's per-plot record and events, both already ruled), then the store hall and walking
  (needs R1's numbers). About 8 PRs.
- **Thin baseline:** the two moving lines and units on the auction board, and a static per-plot list with reasons on
  the phone's harvest screen; no store change, no animation. About 2 PRs, WS3 and WS4 only.
- **Why the baseline loses:** Heiko ruled for the walk-in store (D1 "One hall, two rows", R1 "Yes, sim first") and
  asked for "cool visual representation on the screen (map), nice animations" for events on 2026-10-21; the baseline
  delivers neither. Checkable in SEAMS.md's rulings log and the PM's relays of that day.

## §2 · The Logic `[handoff]`

```
RULE-1 · The two moving lines                                     shape: decision table

inputs
  stage       'walk' | other
  sellers     walkers with side seller and units > 0 (units unknown → counted)
  buyers      walkers with side buyer
  deals       none | some
  marks       {ask, bid} as the caller asks (goods: both; land: bid only)

  #   when                                      then
  1   stage ≠ walk                              no moving lines
  2   deals running                             no moving lines: the trade bar is where they met
  3   marks.ask and sellers exist               ask line at the lowest seller's unrounded price, red, tag on axis
  4   marks.bid and buyers exist                bid line at the highest buyer's unrounded price, green, tag on axis
  5   both lines drawn and ask − bid > 0        label "N APART" between them, at the right edge of the columns

unknown
  units absent on a walker (a caller that doesn't pass C2) means "has units": the line still shows.
outcome
  the words readout beside the board names who holds each line ("LOWEST ASK 70 · Brix").
examples
  Brix asks 70, Ossa 76, Kai bids 61, Lena 58 → ask 70, bid 61, "9 APART"
  Brix and Kai at 63 trading → no lines, trade bar at 63
  land lot, Ossa 310, Lena 290 → bid line at 310 only
```

```
RULE-2 · Picking a side on the status screen (R2, with AM-1)       shape: decision table

when the status screen opens, every walker is seeded with defaultSide (today's rule):
  stock > need → seller · stock < need → buyer · otherwise pass

  #   when                                       then
  1   player taps Buy or Sell                    that side; tapping the chosen side again → pass
  2   player has 0 units                         Sell is disabled
  3   the status clock ends, or all are done     the walk starts with each walker on their side's start edge
  4   during the walk                            switching side works as today (jump to the start edge)

examples
  Lena 0 Smithore, needs 2 → starts as Buy; taps Buy → Pass
  Brix 12, needs 4 → starts as Sell; Buy available
```

## §4 · Failure & Ops `[handoff]`

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| A dependency unavailable? | None external. A month with no `production` in the view (an older server) shows today's Results screen. |
| Retries, poison, paging? | Not applicable: no I/O added. |
| Idempotency? | The show is a pure function of the view and t; a reload mid-show starts it from the phase's elapsed time. |
| Races? | Walking (C13): the server orders arrivals by its clock; two colonists at the corral with one MULE left go by arrival time, then seat order. |
| What works when this is broken? | Every phase still ends on its own clock; the show is display only. |
| How does an operator know? | The e2e screenshot steps (UBER-AC-2) and the news line "Harvest is in" are unchanged. |
