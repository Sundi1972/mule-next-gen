# UX audit: the four priority-1 screens, played as a 14-year-old

Heiko's ask (2026-10-28, via the PM): a deep audit of the planning hub, the election and politics, development and
the goods auction; for each intent in the Claude Doc "Player Intents Per Screen", whether the screen really gets a
newcomer there; proposals where it doesn't; then one mock per changed screen, each showing on screen the intents it
serves and why each change was made. Delivered screen by screen (the PM's pipeline order): hub, election, development,
goods auction.

**Build audited:** #128's head 39ecd76 (ws1-balance 149b2eb, the walking build, plus planning without a clock), served
from a frozen scratch worktree; from the election on, #128 as merged (ws1-balance 8b2caec), which only adds the TV fix the audit found. A sideways phone at 844×390 (the e2e size) and the TV at 1280×720, real speed. Seats:
Mia (the host, played by me), Leo (a second human who never presses anything, played by a bot, so planning waits for
someone) and one AI. Two rooms: one played through month 1, one for the election on purpose.

**Method:** per screen the 5-second first look, the player's own top intents, then each listed intent tried for real:
found or not, taps, where stuck, words not understood; verdict easy / with effort / fails.

**Bias I correct for:** I designed several of these screens, so I know where everything is. I count a scroll or a
desk switch as effort even when I knew to make it, and I judge copy by whether it says what to do, not whether I can
decode it.

## 2 · Planning hub

**First look (5 s).** Month 1 opens with a map, three cards under "THIS MONTH" and a Now card "Run for an office, or
press I'm ready". A newcomer can say "it's the start of the month, I can scout and sell, there's an election", but not
what the month holds after this screen, and not that the election is running on a 20-second clock (see §3).

**Their own top intents** (before reading ours): "what do I do first?", "what is this map, which land is good?", "how
much money do I have and is it a lot?", "who's winning?". The last is not offered anywhere in planning.

### Proposals (the hub mock shows each, with its intent and reason on screen)

- **H-1 · A month roadmap** (intent 1). At the top of the stage: Plan → Land grant → Develop → Produce → Auction, the
  current step lit, and one plain line under it that says what to do now and what comes next ("Look around, scout a
  plot if you like, then press Ready. Next: you pick a free plot."). The offices strip folds into it as a short line.
- **H-2 · Needs on the stage** (intent 2). A compact strip under the roadmap: "Next month you eat · Food 6 of 2 ✓ ·
  Energy 0 of 0 ✓ · Water 6 of 1 ✓", red when short; tapping it opens the Colony desk.
- **H-3 · The drone says what it does** (intent 3). The lock becomes an eye-slash "Only you" chip; while the tool is
  open the phone's rail says "Tap up to 2 free plots"; the button counts "Send the drone · 1 of 2 plots · 60 cr" with a
  "pick one more" hint; the land grant's rail shows "Your drone: 12·5 Desert, good for Energy" when that plot is lit.
- **H-4 · Standings** (intent 6). "You're 2nd of 3" beside the money in the header; tapping it opens a standings sheet
  (rank, name, money, land, goods). The TV's ready list gains each player's rank.
- **H-5 · The colony fund in one card** (intent 7), first in the Colony desk: "Colony fund 0 cr · in: the 5% sales
  tax · out: Governor 50, Sheriff 30 a month".
- **H-6 · Plain words** (cross-cutting): "Next election: month 4"; "Governor: Leo, until month 3"; "The export ship
  calls in month 3: it buys what the colony has left over".
- **H-7 · Room on the phone** (cross-cutting). Once read, the Now card folds to its title (tap to open), so the desk
  tabs and the needs strip are above the fold at 844×390.

## 3 · Election and politics

**How I tried it.** Twice. First at real speed (room JWKB): the stages run 20 s to run, 20 s to vote and 6 s to show
the result, inside planning. While I read the hub, nobody ran and nobody voted, and the game appointed a Governor.
Then deliberately (room FKFQ, on #128 as merged): with the three stage clocks lengthened in my scratch copy only, so
each intent could be tried apart from the clock. The verdicts below say where the real clock changes them. Seats: me
(the host), Leo (a person who presses nothing) and two AIs, The Accountant and The Baron, who both ran for Governor.

**First look (5 s).** "Run for an office, or press I'm ready · Election month: Governor and Sheriff. A Governor
candidate promises something about the tax. Then the secret ballot, then the result on every screen at once." A
newcomer learns that there is an election, not what a Governor is, and not that it is already running on a clock. The
election lives in the Council desk, under the Now card; on the phone you scroll to reach it. The TV shows nothing of it
until the result.

**Their own top intents:** "what is this, do I have to?", "what do I get if I win?", "who should I vote for?".

### Proposals (mock only: these are WS7's screens; the election mock shows each with its intent and reason)

- **E-1 · The election waits for people, like planning** (intents 2, 4; Heiko's "planning should not be time bound").
  Running closes when every person has run or tapped "Not running this time"; the ballot closes when every person has
  voted or abstained; the host can close either for someone who walked away, as with Continue. A rule change (WS7, the
  numbers WS1's), so a decision for Heiko.
- **E-2 · The election takes the stage** (intents 1, 2, 5). While it runs, the stage shows the election instead of the
  map: the three steps, who runs and on what promise, and who we are waiting for. Tapping "Election day" goes there. It
  is never under the Now card in a desk.
- **E-3 · What the offices do, in one card** (intent 1): "Governor: sets the sales tax each month; the tax fills the
  colony fund; paid 50 cr a month. Sheriff: paid 30 cr a month." The Sheriff's job is WS7's to decide.
- **E-4 · A promise reads as a change from today** (intent 5): "up to 8% (it's 5% now)", "lower it", "keep it at 5%";
  shown next to each name while running, and on the TV while running and voting.
- **E-5 · Your votes in words** (intent 6): "You have 1 vote. Everyone gets 1; +1 for every 10 plots; +1 with a good
  name (70+); +1 for every 300 cr of tax you've paid, up to 2." A sentence on the phone, the table on the TV.
- **E-6 · The whole result on the phone** (intent 7): every candidate with their votes, and the tie in plain words
  ("a tie: nobody owns land yet, so the first seat wins"); the news line says the same.
- **E-7 · The Governor's desk keeps the promise in view** (intents 8, 9): "You promised: no higher than 4%" above the
  tax; going past it says "this breaks your promise"; "Set" answers "Done: 4% from now on"; the offices line reads
  "Governor Mia · promised up to 4% · tax 4%, kept". The promise has to outlive the election (WS7).
- **E-8 · The Land Baron always named** (intent 10): "Land Baron: nobody yet. Whoever owns the most plots gets +1
  vote and breaks ties." And an AI not called "The Baron" (WS2's names).
- **E-9 · Fit on the phone**: short button words ("Run · up to 4%", "Switch to Sheriff"); the Governor tab and its desk
  never behind the footer.

For WS7, not the mock: a month-1 tie always goes to the host; the Sheriff does nothing yet.
