Heiko's ask (2026-10-28, via the PM): a deep audit of the planning hub, the election and politics, development and
the goods auction; for each intent in the Claude Doc "Player Intents Per Screen", whether the screen really gets a
newcomer there; proposals where it doesn't; then one mock per changed screen, each showing on screen the intents it
serves and why each change was made. Delivered screen by screen (the PM's pipeline order): hub, election, development,
goods auction.

**Build audited:** #128's head 39ecd76 (ws1-balance 149b2eb, the walking build, plus planning without a clock), served
from a frozen scratch worktree. A sideways phone at 844×390 (the e2e size) and the TV at 1280×720, real speed. Seats:
Mia (the host, played by me), Leo (a second human who never presses anything, played by a bot, so planning waits for
someone) and one AI. Two rooms: one played through month 1, one for the election on purpose.

**Method:** per screen the 5-second first look, the player's own top intents, then each listed intent tried for real:
found or not, taps, where stuck, words not understood; verdict easy / with effort / fails.

**Bias I correct for:** I designed several of these screens, so I know where everything is. I count a scroll or a
desk switch as effort even when I knew to make it, and I judge copy by whether it says what to do, not whether I can
decode it.

## 2 · Planning hub

**First look (5 s).** Month 1 opens with a map, three cards under "THIS MONTH" and a Now card "Run for an office, or
press I'm ready". A newcomer can say "it's the start of the month, I can scout and sell, there's an election", but not
what the month holds after this screen, and not that the election is running on a 20-second clock (see §3).

**Their own top intents** (before reading ours): "what do I do first?", "what is this map, which land is good?", "how
much money do I have and is it a lot?", "who's winning?". The last is not offered anywhere in planning.

### Proposals (the hub mock shows each, with its intent and reason on screen)

- **H-1 · A month roadmap** (intent 1). At the top of the stage: Plan → Land grant → Develop → Produce → Auction, the
  current step lit, and one plain line under it that says what to do now and what comes next ("Look around, scout a
  plot if you like, then press Ready. Next: you pick a free plot."). The offices strip folds into it as a short line.
- **H-2 · Needs on the stage** (intent 2). A compact strip under the roadmap: "Next month you eat · Food 6 of 2 ✓ ·
  Energy 0 of 0 ✓ · Water 6 of 1 ✓", red when short; tapping it opens the Colony desk.
- **H-3 · The drone says what it does** (intent 3). The lock becomes an eye-slash "Only you" chip; while the tool is
  open the phone's rail says "Tap up to 2 free plots"; the button counts "Send the drone · 1 of 2 plots · 60 cr" with a
  "pick one more" hint; the land grant's rail shows "Your drone: 12·5 Desert, good for Energy" when that plot is lit.
- **H-4 · Standings** (intent 6). "You're 2nd of 3" beside the money in the header; tapping it opens a standings sheet
  (rank, name, money, land, goods). The TV's ready list gains each player's rank.
- **H-5 · The colony fund in one card** (intent 7), first in the Colony desk: "Colony fund 0 cr · in: the 5% sales
  tax · out: Governor 50, Sheriff 30 a month".
- **H-6 · Plain words** (cross-cutting): "Next election: month 4"; "Governor: Leo, until month 3"; "The export ship
  calls in month 3: it buys what the colony has left over".
- **H-7 · Room on the phone** (cross-cutting). Once read, the Now card folds to its title (tap to open), so the desk
  tabs and the needs strip are above the fold at 844×390.
