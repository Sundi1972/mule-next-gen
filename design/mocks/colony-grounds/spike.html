The colony grounds: walking colonists, and can every place be seen?

WS10 spike, 8 Nov. Two questions from Heiko: nicer colonists with a real walk, and whether the one fixed view makes any building's place hard to see. Rotation and zoom are parked (zoom is Jira SA-1379).

Can every place be seen in the one view?

Yes, enough to find it: no place is ever fully hidden. Nobody picks a place in the game. When the Governor commissions a building, it takes the first free block nearest the town hall (rule RULE-32b), so a building in front can never get in the way of a choice. What can happen is that a new building's site lands behind a tall one. At worst two thirds of its ground is covered, and its chip on the phone always shows.

How it was measured

Every order the five buildings can be commissioned in (120 orders, 120 different layouts), with every building standing at its tallest stage. Each layout was drawn by the game's own view: the phone desk at 390 px wide, the hub's tab at 353, and the TV opener at 1280. For every place it counts how much of its ground the pictures in front of it cover, using their real transparency. Chips are drawn above every building, so only another chip can cover one.

Which places are worst

  • (1,2), left of the town hall: a new site there is up to two thirds hidden when the Stadium has taken the block in front of it. This is the worst case for a new building.
  • (2,1), right behind the town hall: its ground is mostly behind the hall (median 59 %). It is always the first 1 × 1 place taken, though, and a building standing there rises above the hall.
  • The 2 × 2 blocks (the Stadium and the Spaceport) and the front places: 0–30 %.

If we want the newest site to stand out more

  • Recommended On the TV opener, fade the buildings in front of the place the caption names, to about a third, during the lean-in only. This is in WS10's live layer, about 2 hours with a test.
  • Lift the named place's outline above the buildings, as a soft glowing edge, during the lean-in. It costs about the same as the fade and can be combined with it.
  • The phone needs nothing: the chip shows, and the Colony desk's list names every building with its stage.
  • If players should one day choose places, that is a rule change (WS7, RULE-32b) and needs Heiko's ruling. The same measure then shows which places need help when picking.

Owners: commissioning happens on the phone's Colony desk (WS7's screen). The grounds and their live layer (chips, colonists, glow) are WS10's.
