Walk auction prototype

Hold ▲ or ▼ in the rail (or ↑ ↓) to walk (sound on for the clock and the trade ticks). Sellers start at the top and walk down; buyers start at the bottom and walk up. When you meet someone, units change hands with a tick that speeds up the longer you stay. Walk onto a store line to trade with the store. The clock runs at quarter speed while a trade is on. The other three players are played by the computer, and the numbers are made up.
